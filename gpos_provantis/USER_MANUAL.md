# GPOS Provantis User Manual

This guide is for store staff and cashiers who use the dashboard every day.

It explains the basic steps for selling items, applying discounts, taking payments, using the Others menu, and closing the shift.

The instructions are simple and written for everyday use.

## 1. What the dashboard looks like

When you log in, you will see the dashboard.

The main parts are:

- Product area: where items are shown and selected
- Barcode field: where you scan or type a barcode
- Cart: where the selected items are listed
- Discount button: used to apply a discount
- Total area: shows the amount due
- Charge button: used to finish the sale
- Shift button: used to open or close the shift
- Others button: used for extra actions like reports, cash drop, and printing

## 2. Start a shift

Before taking sales, you must open the shift.

How to do it:

1. Go to the dashboard.
2. Tap the Start shift button.
3. If prompted, enter the opening cash count.
4. Confirm the start.

You are now ready to sell.

When your work period ends, tap End shift to close it.

Important:

- Do not start selling before opening the shift.
- Always count cash before ending the shift.

## 3. Add items to the sale

There are two common ways to add products:

### Option A: Scan a barcode

1. Place the item in front of the barcode scanner or type the barcode in the barcode field.
2. Press Enter or tap Add.
3. The item will appear in the cart.

### Option B: Select from the product list

1. Find the item on the dashboard.
2. Tap the item to add it to the cart.
3. The item will appear in the cart with the quantity.

## 4. Change item quantity

In the cart, you can:

- add more quantity
- remove an item
- reduce the quantity
- delete the item if needed

Use the quantity controls on each item row.

If a customer changes their mind, adjust the cart before payment.

## 5. Apply a discount

To apply a discount:

1. Look at the cart area.
2. Tap the Add discount section.
3. Choose the discount from the list.
4. Confirm the discount.

The discount is then deducted from the total.

You will see the discount amount in the totals area.

If the discount needs customer information, the app may ask for a customer ID or name before applying it.

### Remove a discount

If you need to remove it:

1. Tap the discount area again.
2. Tap Clear or Remove discount.

## 6. Check the sale total

Before charging, review:

- subtotal
- discount
- total amount due

Make sure the customer is charged the correct amount.

## 7. Charge the customer

When everything is correct:

1. Tap Charge.
2. The payment screen will open.
3. Choose the payment method.

You can use:

- Cash
- E-payment
- Split payment

## 8. Cash payment

If the customer pays in cash:

1. Choose Cash.
2. Enter the amount received.
3. Confirm the payment.

The system will calculate:

- total due
- amount received
- change to return

If change is needed, return the correct amount to the customer.

## 9. E-payment

If the customer pays using an electronic payment method:

1. Choose E-Payment.
2. Enter or confirm the payment amount.
3. Confirm the transaction.

The receipt will then be printed if the printer is connected.

## 10. Split payment

Split payment means the customer pays using more than one method.

Example:

- cash part
- card part

How to do it:

1. Tap Split payment.
2. Choose how the total will be divided.
3. Enter the first payment amount.
4. Enter the second payment amount.
5. Confirm the transaction.

The app will check if the total matches the sale.

If the amounts do not match, you must fix them before confirming.

## 11. Print the receipt

Once payment is complete, the receipt is usually printed automatically.

If the printer is working, the customer receives a receipt.

If nothing prints:

- check the printer connection
- confirm the printer is configured
- check if the correct printer is selected

## 12. Use the Others menu

The Others menu contains extra actions used during daily operations.

Tap Others on the dashboard.

Some common options include:

### Reprint receipt

Use this if a customer needs another copy of the receipt.

### Refund

Use this to process a returned item or a refund transaction.

### Send e-receipt

Use this to send the receipt by email if the customer has an email address.

### Cash drop

Use this when you need to remove cash from the drawer and record it safely.

### Open cash drawer

Use this to open the cash drawer manually when needed.

### Cash reports

Use this to check the cash movement and daily cash details.

### Receipts

Use this to view or review recent receipt records.

### Reports

Use this to view sales and shift-related summaries.

### Sold items

Use this to see the items sold in the current period or transaction history.

### Sync data

Use this when you need to sync local data with the system.

### Restart POS

Use this when the terminal needs to restart.

## 13. How to use cash drop

Cash drop is used to remove money from the drawer for safe handling.

How to do it:

1. Tap Others.
2. Tap Cash drop.
3. Enter the amount being dropped.
4. Confirm the action.

The system records the drop and prints a slip if required.

## 14. How to open the cash drawer

If the drawer needs to be opened manually:

1. Tap Others.
2. Tap Open cash drawer.
3. Confirm the action.

This is useful when you need to remove or count cash.

## 15. How to view receipts or reports

From the Others menu:

1. Tap Receipts or Reports.
2. Review the list.
3. Open the item you need.

These screens help you check customer receipts and sales history.

## 16. How to handle refunds

If a customer wants a refund:

1. Tap Others.
2. Tap Refund.
3. Follow the instructions on the screen.
4. Confirm the refund.

Make sure the refund is approved before you complete it.

## 17. How to send an e-receipt

If the customer wants a digital receipt:

1. Tap Others.
2. Tap Send e-receipt.
3. Enter the customer email if required.
4. Send the receipt.

This is useful when the customer does not want a printed receipt.

## 18. End of shift

At the end of the work period:

1. Tap End shift.
2. Count the drawer if asked.
3. Confirm the closing process.

This closes the shift and records the sales summary.

Do this before leaving the terminal.

## 19. Basic sales flow summary

A simple sale usually goes like this:

1. Open shift
2. Scan or add items
3. Adjust quantity if needed
4. Apply discount if needed
5. Tap Charge
6. Choose payment method
7. Enter payment amount
8. Confirm payment
9. Receipt prints
10. Continue with the next customer
11. End shift at the end of the day

## 20. Common cashier tips

- Always open the shift before selling.
- Check the cart before charging.
- Make sure the total is correct before confirming.
- Always confirm the payment method and exchange amount.
- If a printer is not working, check settings before continuing.
- If you are unsure, ask a supervisor.

## 21. Common issues and what to do

### The item does not show up after scanning

- Make sure the barcode is correct.
- Check that the product is available.
- Try searching for the item manually.

### The customer wants a discount

- Tap the discount button.
- Choose the correct discount.
- Confirm it before charging.

### The customer wants to pay partly in cash and partly by card

- Use Split payment.
- Enter both amounts.
- Confirm the split total matches the sale.

### No receipt printed

- Check the printer connection.
- Check if the printer is configured.
- Try a test print.

### Shift cannot be opened or closed

- Check if the cash count is required.
- Make sure the shift is not already open or closed.
- Ask a supervisor if you are unsure.

## 22. Quick reminder

The main steps are:

- Open shift
- Add items
- Apply discount if needed
- Charge
- Choose payment method
- Print receipt
- End shift at the end of the day

This is the basic workflow for everyday POS use.

## 23. Final note

If you follow the steps above, you can complete most cashier tasks quickly and correctly.

For anything more advanced, ask a supervisor or use the Reports and Others sections to review sales and payment details.
